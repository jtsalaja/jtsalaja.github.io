## Overview

Used Python (NumPy, Matplotlib) to build a compartmental (SIR-style) model of the 2009 influenza outbreak at the U.S. Air Force Academy, solving systems of ordinary differential equations to simulate disease spread through a population.

## What it explored

Transmission rates, recovery periods, and intervention scenarios, laying groundwork for the compartmental-modeling approach I later applied to my senior thesis on fare-free transit policy.
