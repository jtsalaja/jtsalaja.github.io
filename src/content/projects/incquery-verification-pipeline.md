## Context

Built during a summer as an Industrial Research Intern with IncQuery and a RIPS-NSF Scholar at UCLA's Institute for Pure and Applied Mathematics (IPAM).

## What I built

An automated verification pipeline that formalizes all 42 operations in IncQuery's structured survey-logic language, enabling automated checks for unreachable questions and infeasible quotas across any survey built on the platform, catching design errors before a survey ever goes live.

## Impact

Co-authored the project's technical report and presented findings to 20 IncQuery engineers and 50+ attendees at IPAM's public research Project Day, translating the formal verification work for both specialist and general audiences.
